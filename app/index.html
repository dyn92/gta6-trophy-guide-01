    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough, then a clean-up. This roadmap is built from the official trophy descriptions alone — which chapters and missions the trophies name, which collectibles and counts they carry — in the order that wastes the least travel. Stage progress fills in from your checklist.

    Missables & one-chance moments

    Most of the list cannot be missed: chapter and mission trophies are completions, and everything else is a count or a free-roam feat you can go back to. Three trophies are different — their official descriptions name a single scene or a single attempt. Until the community confirms a way to repeat them, treat these as one-chance and use a spare save slot around them.

    How this list was made

    We flagged a trophy only when its official description describes one unrepeatable moment rather than a completion, a count or a place you can revisit. Everything not flagged here is, on the wording of its description, earnable at any time — story trophies included, since each one is simply “complete” a named chapter or mission. If a later patch or a community finding changes that picture, the flag list is the first thing to update.

    About this guide & its sources

    Leonida Platinum Atlas is a free, open-source, unofficial fan guide. It runs entirely from its own files: open index.html in a desktop browser with the network off and everything — the checklist, the roadmap, this page — still works. Your progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and can be exported to a file and imported back on another machine.

    Where the facts come from

    Every trophy's name, official description and grade are PlayStation's, as published in the official Grand Theft Auto VI list on Exophase (exophase.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi-psn/trophies/). Where PlayStation tracks a count for a trophy — 250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range challenges, 100 WAiNK spots — the target shown is the one PSNProfiles lists for it (psnprofiles.com/trophies/43531-grand-theft-auto-vi). Each trophy's own guide names these sources again beside it.

    What is ours, and what we have not checked

    The guide text under each trophy is our own writing, worked out from the official description: what the trophy asks for, where its description points, and when it can be missed. The game launched the week this guide was written and we have not verified every step in play, so each guide says plainly where a place or a step is not yet pinned down rather than inventing one. The roadmap and the missable flags are likewise built from the official descriptions, and the Missables page explains the rule we used. No text here is copied from another guide, and the look is our own — there is no Rockstar Games logo, artwork or game screenshot anywhere in this app.

    The spoiler setting

    Twenty-eight of the 55 trophies are hidden on PlayStation until earned. Turn on Hide story spoilers in the checklist and every unearned story trophy is masked — name, description and guide — until you tick it off, so you can track the hunt without reading the campaign's beats ahead of time.
